# Code of Conduct

Kairn contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive in reviews and technical discussion.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Do not engage in harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or sustained disruption.
- Respect private information and coordinated security disclosures.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's GitHub contact or security-advisory channel. The maintainer may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants whose conduct harms the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and delegated where possible.
